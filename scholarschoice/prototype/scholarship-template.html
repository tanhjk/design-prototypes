Requirements

Who can apply

  • Singapore Citizens, or Permanent Residents willing to take up citizenship
  • Students in their final year of A-levels, IB, polytechnic or equivalent
  • Undergraduates in their first or second year of a relevant degree
  • Male applicants must have completed or be able to defer National Service

Academic results

  • Good A-level, IB or polytechnic results, with strength in mathematics and the sciences
  • Undergraduates: a strong grade point average in their current degree
  • A record of leadership or co-curricular involvement

What to prepare

  • Results slips for secondary and pre-university examinations
  • Testimonials and co-curricular records
  • University offer letters or applications, if available

How selection works

  1. Online application Submit your application and documents through the provider’s portal.
  2. Assessment Shortlisted applicants sit psychometric tests and a group exercise.
  3. Interview A panel interview with senior DSTA engineers and leaders.
  4. Offer Successful applicants hear back within placeholder weeks.

Part 02

Value

Successful applicants will be given the following support* for up to 6 years:

  • Full tuition fees
  • Monthly sustenance allowance, inclusive of CPF contribution (where applicable)
  • Monthly overseas living allowance
  • Annual return airfare

Other allowances

  • Conference allowance
  • Thesis allowance
  • Computer allowance
  • Settling-in allowance
  • Language programme fees (for scholars studying in non-anglophone countries, where applicable)

Mid-term NSS (PhD) awardees will be offered the same terms of support as the full-term NSS (PhD) awardees from the point of the award, with retrospective payment for all tuition fees and/or any compulsory fees from Year 1 of study onwards.

Service commitment

  • Upon completion of the PhD programme, scholars will return to Singapore and contribute to A*STAR’s research institutes and/or relevant organisations for 4 years.
  • Scholars who complete their PhD in a non-anglophone country would serve a 3-year service commitment instead.

*All rates/allowances vary according to country and university, and are subject to adjustments made by A*STAR.
